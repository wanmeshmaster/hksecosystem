App Store Help Center

Answers to common questions about accounts, installs, and permissions. Can't find what you need? Reach out to contact@hkmail.cn.

Use the "Create Account" button in the top right of the App Store. You'll need an existing HKMail address — we send a verification code there to confirm it's you before your account is created.

Check your HKMail inbox (and spam folder) for a message from App Store. If it hasn't arrived after a minute, use "Resend code" on the verification screen.

App Store permissions follow your HKMail account. If you're an administrator in HKMail, that same status carries over here automatically — there's nothing separate to request or set up.

You can browse listings without signing in, but creating an account and installing apps requires a valid HKMail address, since that's what the App Store uses for sign-in and verification.

The full App Store Terms & Conditions are linked at the bottom of the App Store page, or you can go straight to the Terms page.
